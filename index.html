About

Evidence-led programme professional with a broad management trajectory.

I am a PMP-, Project DPro- and MEAL DPro-certified humanitarian and development professional with more than 16 years of cross-sector experience across NGOs, humanitarian WASH, public health, education, research, private-sector operations and compliance.

My core strength is connecting programme evidence with management action. I have designed and strengthened monitoring systems, managed indicator tracking and data-quality processes, led and supported assessments, developed digital monitoring tools, improved information management, coordinated corrective actions, supervised teams and field personnel, and supported evidence-based reporting and programme-quality decisions.

My humanitarian experience in Cox’s Bazar has strengthened my practical understanding of WASH programme quality, accountability, community feedback, safeguarding, inclusion and field-level implementation challenges. Earlier private-sector experience in EHS, audit follow-up, risk assessment, safety systems and continuous improvement adds an operational discipline that I apply to programme quality and risk management.
